# MediGuide-AI: Clinical Document Intelligence & Human-in-the-Loop Safety Platform

**AMD Developer Hackathon — ACT III: Health & Wellbeing**

---

## 1. Executive Summary

**MediGuide-AI** is a healthcare document intelligence and clinical safety platform engineered to bridge the gap between complex diagnostic lab reports and patient comprehension. By combining on-premise local biomedical Natural Language Processing (NLP) with deterministic safety guardrails, SQLite persistence, role-based access control (RBAC), and human clinical escalation workflows, MediGuide-AI ensures that healthcare information is both accessible to patients and rigorously validated by licensed healthcare professionals.

In health and wellbeing technology, automated intelligence cannot operate in an unverified black box. MediGuide-AI enforces a strict **human-in-the-loop** architecture: whenever critical clinical thresholds, drug-drug interactions, or high-risk vital signs (e.g., severe hypertension, acute chest pain) are detected, the system automatically routes the case to a physician review queue before presenting actionable guidance to patients.

---

## 2. The Problem: The Clinical Comprehension Gap & Automated Risk

1. **High Patient Anxiety & Misinterpretation**: Diagnostic reports, pathology results, and discharge summaries are densely formatted with technical jargon, abbreviations, and clinical units. Patients frequently misinterpret reference ranges or search the internet, causing panic or delayed care.
2. **Hallucination Dangers of Pure LLMs**: Unconstrained generative language models frequently hallucinate clinical facts, invent dosages, or provide unvalidated medical advice.
3. **Absence of Governance & Persistence**: Many hackathon prototypes rely on transient in-memory stores, lack audit trails, expose patient records across account boundaries, or discard risk alerts silently in the frontend.

---

## 3. The MediGuide-AI Solution

MediGuide-AI introduces a robust, multi-stage software architecture divided into two clear operational tiers:

### Tier 1: The Frozen Local AI Pipeline
- **Document Processing**: PyMuPDF for native digital PDFs, coupled with an automatic PaddleOCR fallback engine for scanned paper documents and images.
- **Biomedical Named Entity Recognition (NER)**: `d4data/biomedical-ner-all` token classification model identifying symptoms, anatomical markers, medications, and diagnostic tests.
- **Summarization**: `sshleifer/distilbart-cnn-6-6` sequence-to-sequence model fine-tuned for extracting key clinical findings.
- **Deterministic Verification Guardrails**: Post-summarization numeric metric validation ensuring numbers, blood pressure values, and clinical units in the summary match extracted facts exactly. If verification fails, a deterministic template fallback is activated instantly.
- **Hardware Profile**: Runs 100% locally on standard CPU environments with zero dependence on paid external cloud APIs, designed to be forward-compatible with AMD ROCm and AMD Instinct accelerators.

### Tier 2: Safe Downstream Persistence & Clinical Review
- **ACID SQLite Persistence**: Permanent relational schema (`users`, `reports`, `review_requests`, `audit_logs`) with Write-Ahead Logging (WAL) and strict foreign key integrity.
- **Server-Side Authentication & RBAC**: PBKDF2 password hashing with salt, cryptographically signed HMAC-SHA256 Bearer tokens, and server-enforced role separation (`patient`, `doctor`, `admin`).
- **Patient Data Isolation**: Patients can strictly only view their own uploaded records. Direct ID access to other patients' reports returns HTTP 403 Forbidden.
- **Human Clinical Review Workflow**: Automated rule-based risk classification (`LOW`, `MEDIUM`, `HIGH`, `UNCERTAIN`). High-risk cases are escalated directly to certified clinicians who can inspect findings, document clinical notes, sign off with professional credentials, and archive reviews.
- **Doctor & Administrative Governance**: User account lifecycle management, status suspension/reactivation, and live telemetry tracking system uptime and model readiness.

## 4. Safety & Non-Diagnostic Medical Disclaimers

> ### **CRITICAL MEDICAL DISCLAIMER**
> MediGuide-AI is an educational and informational tool designed to assist patients in understanding diagnostic reports and organizing questions for their healthcare providers.
> - **NOT A MEDICAL DEVICE**: MediGuide-AI does not provide medical diagnoses, clinical prognoses, treatment recommendations, or medication dosages.
> - **DO NOT DELAY MEDICAL CARE**: Never disregard professional medical advice or delay seeking treatment because of information generated by MediGuide-AI.
> - **EMERGENCY SITUATIONS**: If you believe you are experiencing a medical emergency (e.g., severe chest pain, sudden numbness, difficulty breathing), immediately contact emergency medical services (such as 911 or your local emergency number) or go to the nearest emergency department.
> - **SYNTHETIC TEST DATA**: All reports, cases, and patient identities utilized in automated tests, benchmarks, and demonstration scripts are entirely synthetic and anonymized. No protected health information (PHI) is stored or retained.
